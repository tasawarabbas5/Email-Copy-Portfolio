# CTAs & P.S.

## CTA button
- Start with a verb; say what happens next: "Get my template", "See the plan"
- 2-5 words; first person ("my") often lifts clicks
- Avoid "Submit", "Click here", "Learn more" when something specific fits
- Repeat the same CTA in a text link near the end; keep the destination the same

## Reducing friction
Add a line under the button: "Takes 2 minutes", "No credit card", "Cancel anytime".

## P.S.
The P.S. is among the most-read parts. Use it for one of: the offer deadline, a bonus, a guarantee, or a one-line restatement of the CTA.
