# Sequences

For each sequence state: trigger, goal, number of emails, spacing, exit condition.

## Welcome (4-5 emails, days 0, 1, 3, 5, 7)
1. Deliver the promise + set expectations
2. Origin story / why you
3. Best content or quick win
4. Proof / case study
5. Soft offer

## Launch (5-7 emails over 7-10 days)
Teaser, story, offer open, objection/FAQ, proof, deadline (24h), last call (hours left).

## Abandoned cart (3 emails: 1h, 24h, 72h)
Reminder, objection handling + proof, urgency or small incentive.

## Nurture
Rotate education, story, proof, and offer at roughly 3:1 value-to-pitch.

## Re-engagement (2-3 emails)
"Still interested?" preview, best-of content, last chance before unsubscribe.

Always map each email to one job and avoid repeating the same angle.
