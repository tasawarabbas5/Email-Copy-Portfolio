# Subject Lines & Preview Text

## Principles
- 30-50 characters; front-load the key word (mobile truncates)
- Curiosity or clear benefit, never both vague and clickbait
- The subject and preview work as one sentence; do not repeat each other
- Match the promise: the email must deliver what the subject implies

## Angles (offer one of each when giving options)
1. **Benefit**: "Cut your onboarding time in half"
2. **Curiosity**: "The mistake behind most failed launches"
3. **Urgency / scarcity**: "Ends tonight: your 20% code"
4. **Personal / plain**: "Quick question about your store"
5. **Number / list**: "3 fixes for a slow checkout"
6. **Social proof**: "How Maya doubled her list in 30 days"

## Avoid
ALL CAPS, excess punctuation, spammy words (free!!!, act now), fake "Re:" or "Fwd:".

## Preview text
40-90 characters. Add a new detail, a benefit, or a soft CTA.
