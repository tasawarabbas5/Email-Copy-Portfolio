# Email Audit Checklist

Score each item 0-2 (0 = missing, 1 = weak, 2 = strong). Total /20.

1. Subject line earns the open and fits mobile length
2. Preview text adds new information
3. Opening hook is relevant to the reader
4. Single clear goal
5. Reader-focused (you > we)
6. Specific proof and details
7. Objection addressed
8. Scannable formatting
9. One primary CTA, clear and specific
10. P.S. / closing reinforces the action

Output: score, top 3 fixes ranked by impact, then a rewritten version of the weakest part.
