# Body Copy

## Opening hook (first 1-2 lines)
Pick one: a sharp question, a bold claim, a short story, a shared pain, or a surprising fact. Never open with "I hope this finds you well" or the company's history.

## Structures
- **PAS**: Problem, Agitate, Solution
- **AIDA**: Attention, Interest, Desire, Action
- **BAB**: Before, After, Bridge
- **Story, lesson, offer**: best for nurture and newsletters

## Craft
- Lead with the reader's situation, not your product
- Features become benefits become outcomes ("so you can...")
- Handle one objection per email (price, time, trust, fit)
- Use proof close to the claim: a quote, number, or case
- Scannable: short paragraphs, occasional bold, bullets for 3+ items
- Read it aloud; cut any sentence you would not say
- Length follows the ask: low-stakes click = short; high-stakes purchase = longer
